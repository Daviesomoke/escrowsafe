About SecureEscrow Kenya

Founded in 2026, SecureEscrow Kenya addresses the trust deficit in online transactions across the country. We provide a neutral, regulated platform that protects both buyers and sellers.

Our Purpose

The growth of e‑commerce and online classifieds in Kenya has created new opportunities but also new risks. We exist to eliminate the uncertainty in transactions between strangers, ensuring that funds are only released when both parties fulfill their obligations.

How We Protect Your Money

Every transaction is secured with cryptographically hashed access tokens, verified M-PESA payment confirmations, and strict data protections - see our Compliance page for the full technical detail on how your funds and data are actually protected.

Hashed Access Tokens

Every transaction link uses a one-way cryptographic token, never a guessable ID

Verified Payments

Every M-PESA confirmation is cross-checked against the transaction before funds are marked secured

M‑PESA Integration

Built on Safaricom's official Daraja API for payment and payout
