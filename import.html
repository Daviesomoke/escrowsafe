How An Import Order Works

SecureEscrow is the one importing, shipping and handling your device — end to end

01

You Order & Pay Into Escrow

Pick a phone and pay the listed price. Your money goes into SecureEscrow's protected trust account — not to any third party.

02

SecureEscrow Sources & Imports It

We purchase, ship and clear customs on your device ourselves. SecureEscrow is responsible for it from the United States all the way to Kenya — you'll get SMS updates as it progresses.

03

Delivered To You

Your phone is delivered to the address you provided. Inspect it before confirming.

04

You Confirm, Funds Release

Happy with it? Confirm via the secure link sent to your phone and funds are released. Not happy? Raise a dispute instead — SecureEscrow stands behind the device it imported for you.
