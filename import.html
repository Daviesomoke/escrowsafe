How An Import Order Works

The same escrow protection you'd get on any transaction — applied to your import

01

You Order & Pay Into Escrow

Pick a phone and pay the listed price. Your money goes straight into SecureEscrow's protected account — not to us directly.

02

We Source & Import It

We handle sourcing, shipping and customs clearance. You'll get SMS updates as your order progresses.

03

Delivered To You

Your phone is delivered to the address you provided. Inspect it before confirming.

04

You Confirm, Funds Release

Happy with it? Confirm via the secure link sent to your phone and funds are released to us. Not happy? Raise a dispute instead.
