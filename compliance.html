4. How We Actually Protect Your Money and Data

Rather than describe compliance frameworks we haven't yet obtained, here's what SecureEscrow Kenya technically does to protect every transaction:

  • Every transaction is tracked by a unique, randomly generated identifier - not a guessable sequence
  • Access to a transaction's details and actions requires a secret one-time link (magic link) sent directly to the buyer's or seller's phone - not a password you have to remember or that could be guessed
  • These access tokens are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes in our database - even if our database were ever compromised, the tokens themselves could not be recovered or reused
  • Phone numbers and payment details are masked in any view that hasn't been verified with a valid access token
  • All traffic between your browser and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS)
  • Automated rate-limiting is in place across the platform to slow down abuse attempts
  • Funds paid into escrow via M-PESA are held against your specific transaction until you confirm delivery - see Section 8 for how this works technically

We do not currently hold funds in a bank-administered trust account structure, and we are not a Central Bank of Kenya-licensed payment service provider. We are actively reviewing what registration is appropriate for our scale of operations with legal counsel.

8. M-PESA Integration

Payments into and out of escrow run through Safaricom's official M-PESA Daraja API. Specifically:

  • Paying into escrow: when you start a transaction, you receive a genuine M-PESA STK Push prompt directly from Safaricom on your phone. You enter your own M-PESA PIN to confirm - we never see, ask for, or store your PIN at any point.
  • Payment confirmation: Safaricom sends us a signed confirmation once your payment is received; we cross-check the confirmed amount against the transaction before marking funds as secured, so a mismatched or tampered confirmation can't be used to falsely mark a transaction as paid.
  • Releasing funds: for sellers who choose M-PESA as their payout method, release is sent directly to their phone via Safaricom's B2C API once you confirm delivery. (Till and Paybill payouts are processed manually, since Safaricom's automated payout API does not support paying those directly.)
  • We comply with Safaricom's M-PESA API Terms of Service and applicable transaction limits.

If your concern is about data privacy specifically, you're also entitled to contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) directly.

11. Security Measures in Place Today

Rather than claim audits we haven't undergone, here's a straightforward summary of what's actually protecting your data and transactions right now:

  • All access tokens are cryptographically hashed, never stored in readable form
  • Payment confirmations are cross-verified against expected transaction amounts before being trusted
  • Rate limiting is applied across the platform to slow down automated abuse
  • All traffic is encrypted end-to-end (HTTPS)
  • Unverified visitors can never see full phone numbers or payout details for a transaction that isn't theirs
